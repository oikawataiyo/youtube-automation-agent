Avoidant attachment is not coldness. It is love that learned to expect the door to close, so it leaves first. That does not make the hurt less real for the person waiting on the other side. But it does make the pattern understandable enough to interrupt. The strategy was smart once. It read the room correctly in a smaller life, with fewer choices and less language. It is just running in the wrong decade now. So the work is not to shame the part of you that pulls away. The work is to notice when it starts driving, thank it for getting you here, and then choose the harder thing: staying present long enough to find out whether this time is different. If this helped you understand your own distance, send it to someone who keeps confusing withdrawal with indifference. And I will see you in the next one.
