The anxious brain is not broken. It is a forecaster that loves you too aggressively and trusts the worst data because worst data used to be useful. You cannot stop it from predicting. Prediction is what brains do. But you can change what it learns from. You can stop treating every alarm as a command. You can stop making relief the only teacher. You can let the body rehearse danger while you practice staying in the room long enough for a different ending to register. Anxiety may always speak in forecasts. The work is learning which forecasts deserve obedience, and which ones only need to be heard while you keep living.
