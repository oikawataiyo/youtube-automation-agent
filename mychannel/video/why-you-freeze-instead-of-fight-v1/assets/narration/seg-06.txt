The freeze was never the moment you failed. It was the moment your oldest survival system decided it could keep you alive better than your conscious self could. Kozlowska, Walker, McLean and Carrive (2015, Harvard Review of Psychiatry) gave that descent a clinical map, and Roelofs (2017, Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B) showed that stillness can be organized, alert, and purposeful. Knowing that does not make you fragile. It ends a war you were fighting against your own brainstem. The next time you replay the moment and call it cowardice, try a more honest sentence. My body chose the oldest option it had. If this helped you name something you have been blaming yourself for, send it to someone who still thinks freezing means weakness. And I will see you in the next one.
