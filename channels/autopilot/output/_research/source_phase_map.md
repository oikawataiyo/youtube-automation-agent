> Diagnostic of `data/scripts/1779639127625_survivorship-bias-is-ruining-your-decisions.json`
> against [[SCRIPT_FRAMEWORK]] Phase 0-6 + hook/reversal/evidence/curiosity/meta layers.

Source stats: 1,703 words, ~9 minutes, 5 sections + hook + outro, 9 academic references.

## Phase-by-phase diagnosis

### Phase 0 — Hook (0–60s)

**Current text:** "Look at this airplane. Every red dot is a bullet hole. The military said: armor the planes where the holes are. They were exactly wrong — and you're making the same mistake right now."

**Type identification:**
- Closest fits: **H9 衝撃命題先出し型** ("they were exactly wrong") + **H7 「説明できない」矛盾指摘型** ("you're making the same mistake right now" — visceral but unspecified)
- Visual-led, single sentence opens with imperative ("Look at this airplane") — strong attention capture
- Length: 53 words ≈ 22 seconds — well inside 0-60s budget

**Strengths:**
- Strong visual anchor (Wald's diagram) — operates as E1 映像微細分析 even in audio
- "exactly wrong" + "right now" pivot binds historical content to present self → 認知的不協和 immediate
- Single image, 3 beats. Very tight.

**Weaknesses:**
- No explicit **逆張り命題 (H1)** — viewer doesn't know what conventional belief is being overturned yet
- No "remain-to-the-end" promise (Phase 0 requires "最後まで見る理由" within first 30s)
- No 「実は〇〇ではない」-equivalent — the reversal is implicit
- For 20-30 min run, hook needs to promise enough scale to keep viewer through 4-5 reveals; current promises 1

**Verdict:** △ Strong as a 9-min hook. Needs a second beat for 20-30 min.

### Phase 1 — 共有認識の確認 (1–5 min)

**Current text:** Section 1 "The Planes That Came Back" — embeds the common belief implicitly: that the military's instinct to armor where the holes are was reasonable.

**Type identification:**
- Common belief is *enacted* (military's decision) rather than *named* ("many of you think...")
- Phase 1 principle from [[SCRIPT_FRAMEWORK]]: "視聴者が「自分はこれを知っている」と感じる状態を作り、後の反転の落差を最大化する"

**Strengths:**
- Story-first delivery is engaging
- Common-sense logic (armor the holes) is so intuitive that viewer endorses it before reversal

**Weaknesses:**
- The **viewer's** common belief is never named directly. The phase silently piggybacks on the military's belief.
- "視聴者の感想を否定せず、その感想が生まれる構造を説明する" is missing — no explicit step where the script says "you'd probably think the same — here's why that intuition exists"
- The common belief is about WWII, not about the viewer's own decisions. Phase 1 should mirror the viewer's life first to maximize the drop.

**Verdict:** △ Implicit, ride-along Phase 1. Could be much stronger if we mirror the viewer's life *before* Wald.

---

### Phase 2 — 第1反転 (5–15 min)

**Current text:** Wald's reveal inside Section 1 + Section 2 "Why Your Brain Can't See What Isn't There"

**Type identification:**
- **R4 統計による常識崩壊型** — Wald's correction is fundamentally statistical (selection bias)
- **R2 人物像反転型** secondary — Wald himself is the unexpected hero

**Strengths:**
- Wald is named, dated, institutionally located (Columbia Statistical Research Group, 1943) — strong **E3** equivalent
- "you can't unsee it" line lands the unfair-knowledge gift
- Tversky & Kahneman 1973 cited in Cognitive Psychology — proper **E9 実験データ引用**

**Weaknesses:**
- Brown et al 1995 (0.5-1.5% inflation) is delivered as raw fact, no setup/payoff timing — violates E9 best practice of "感覚的な常識を先に確認してからデータを出す"
- No **C2 「後で説明する」先送り** — every evidence is used immediately and exhausted
- No **C5 対立軸** — pro-success-stories camp is never given voice to be refuted
- 「実はですね」-equivalent boundary marker is absent (English equivalent could be "Here's the thing that nobody tells you" or "And here's what changes everything")

**Verdict:** ○ R4/R2 hits well. Speed-control and curiosity scaffolding missing.

---

### Phase 3 — 証拠の積み上げ (15–30 min)

**Current text:** Section 3 "The Industries Built To Hide the Holes" — Denrell 2003, self-help industry, social media

**Type identification:**
- Evidence types used: **E6 第三者研究** (Denrell), **E9 実験データ** (referenced fund returns), **E7 民俗学/科学的典拠** (availability heuristic frame)
- **Phase 3 principle**: 物証 → 文献 → 発言 → 構造的証拠 の順で積む

**Strengths:**
- Wide evidentiary spread — academic + industry + social media
- Each layer adds a different observable surface (books, influencers, algorithms)

**Weaknesses:**
- Order is wrong per Okada framework: jumps to 構造的 evidence (industry structures) before establishing 文献/発言
- No **C10 残り枚数/時間進捗公開** — viewer doesn't know how deep the rabbit hole goes
- No **L7 エスカレーション** structure (each layer should feel like a step up in invisibility)
- No 脱線 — entire section is on-rails

**Verdict:** △ Material is there but ordered for English essay logic, not Okada speed control.

---

### Phase 4 — 第2反転 (30–45 min)

**Current text:** Section 4 "The Decisions This Is Ruining Right Now" — applies bias to viewer's career, relationships, investing, risk-taking

**Type identification:**
- Closest: **R6 美談→構造批判型** ("inspiration without base rates is a coin toss... repackaged and sold to you as a strategy")
- Bessembinder 2018 4% statistic is strong **E9**

**Strengths:**
- Pivot from history → viewer is correctly placed at Phase 4
- "inspiration without base rates is a coin toss someone happened to win" is a memorable independent concept (X独自概念の命名 territory)

**Weaknesses:**
- This is NOT a true Phase 4 第2反転 — it's an application of Phase 2 to new domains
- Phase 4 should "第1反転を否定するのではなく、それも含んだより大きな構造に昇格させる" — the script doesn't escalate to a larger structure
- No **C8 自己更新** moment — narrator never says "I was fooled by X for years"
- No **多段階連鎖** even though the material (career → relationships → investing → risk) wants to be a 4-step R7

**Verdict:** △ Acts as Phase 4 by position, but content is Phase 2 expansion. Missing actual escalation.

---

### Phase 5 — meta connection (45 min+)

**Current text:** Section 5 "How To See What Isn't There" — practical techniques (base rate, graveyard analysis, pre-mortem, falsification)

**Type identification:**
- This is **C12 処方/アクションアイテム** territory
- NO explicit meta connection. The script stays inside decision-making, never scales to civilization / evolution / philosophy
- Closing Kahneman "narrative fallacy" reference gestures at M2/M4 but doesn't develop it

**Strengths:**
- Klein 2007 pre-mortem (HBR) is a strong **E9** citation
- Wald correction as recurring frame is good — "Wald's correction is still the technique" loops back

**Weaknesses:**
- Missing the entire 第3反転 (大) — no scale-shift to "this isn't just about decisions, it's about how human cognition itself evolved"
- **M4 進化論的大枠** is the obvious adjacent meta connection: "your pattern-recognition is doing exactly what it was built to do" — the script *hints* at this in Phase 2 but never elevates it
- No **M7 媒体批評** — could note the irony that this video, by being on a platform, is *itself* a survivorship-bias artifact (only successful videos get watched)

**Verdict:** ✗ Major gap. The script stops at "do these 4 things" without ever stepping back to ask why the brain can't be retrained.

---

### Phase 6 — 終盤 (closing)

**Current text:** Outro — "treat every success story as a partial dataset... who is missing from this story?"

**Strengths:**
- Final question "who is missing from this story?" plants a Zeigarnik loop — good
- Compact, memorable

**Weaknesses:**
- No "次の謎の提示" — does not seed a follow-up video
- No comment/aukart prompt (**C7 参加型**)
- CTA is share-driven, not engagement-driven

**Verdict:** ○ Functions, but doesn't compound across videos.
