You are not broken for feeling alone inside a crowd of notifications. Cacioppo and Patrick would call that ache a signal, not a verdict, and Deci and Ryan would say relatedness is part of what a human life needs in order to function. The cruel trick of modern connection is that it can surround the signal with almost-answers. Views. Stories. Feeds. Little sparks that look warm from a distance. Once you know what loneliness is asking for, you can stop feeding it pictures of contact and start giving it moments of contact. If this helped you name the feeling, send it to one person instead of just saving it. I will see you in the next one.
