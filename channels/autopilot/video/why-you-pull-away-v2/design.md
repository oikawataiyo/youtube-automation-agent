# Design System — why-you-pull-away (Avoidant Attachment)

## Thesis the visuals must encode
Avoidant attachment: the moment closeness arrives, the body cools and retreats — not
because the person doesn't want connection, but because an old circuit learned that
closeness equals danger. Every segment puts that idea on screen literally (Okada PRINCIPLES
原則2 = concept put on screen). The recurring physical grammar is **approach → freeze →
retreat**, and the recurring object is **a door** (closeness available but not crossed).

## Rendering architecture (Hybrid)
- **3D WebGL (three.js)** for Hook / S1 / S2 / S3 / S4 / Outro — chibi figures (two-head-tall
  primitives), rooms, doors, warm↔cold key light. Each composition drives a pose proxy via
  GSAP and renders inside the timeline `onUpdate` (deterministic, `preserveDrawingBuffer`).
- **2D SVG** for S5 (the blueprint) — a pencil tracing a new doorway onto an architectural
  plan. Line-art reads better flat; no 3D needed for the reframe-as-redrawing beat.

## Palette
Warmth that can grade toward cold slate and back — cooling is retreat, re-warming is repair.

## Type stack
Captions: "Helvetica Neue", Arial, 43px, karaoke fill `--mute` → `--ink`, read from
`window.segNN` (generated by `_transcribe.py`). Display titles heavy-weight where a segment
names a concept on screen.

## Motion grammar
- Reach pivots from the shoulder (parent→child rotation); torso leans from the pelvis.
- Light color lerps `--warm` ↔ `--cold` driven by a single proxy value (`warm` 1↔0).
- The **door** is the through-line: ajar in Hook, closed in S1/S2, knocked-on in S4,
  redrawn in S5, opening slightly in Outro (warmth returns — the reframe).
- No static hold > 15s; subtle continuous motion (breath, dust, light drift) per shot.

## Per-segment register
| seg | render | still life (from script visual_note) | concept put on screen |
|-----|--------|----------------------------------------|------------------------|
| 00 hook | 3D | two cups on a table, one chair pushed back, a hand reaching that stops halfway, a door left slightly open | approach → freeze → retreat; closeness arrives, body goes cold |
| 01 | 3D | nursery at dusk, small child beside a closed bedroom door, toys in perfect rows, a pulse of light under the floorboards | the early learning — closeness met a closed door; order as self-soothing |
| 02 | 3D | grown bedroom dissolves into a childhood room, same doorway in both, adult hand hovering above a phone without sending | the old room overlaid on the present; the unsent message |
| 03 | 3D | desk of warm photos of two people, one hand turning each photo face down until the tabletop is blank | pre-emptive withdrawal — erasing closeness before it can hurt |
| 04 | 3D | dim hallway, a person outside a closed apartment door (hand lowered after knocking), another inside pressed against the wood | both sides want in; the door between them is the avoidance |
| 05 | 2D SVG | a pencil tracing an old blueprint — not erasing the first lines, adding a new doorway where a wall used to be | the reframe — you don't delete the wall, you draw a door |
| 06 outro | 3D | callback to Hook: the door opens slightly, warmth returns to the table | repair — closeness can be approached, slowly |

## HyperFrames invariants (see CLAUDE.md)
Each segment is a **standalone** composition (own HTML, own `data-composition-id="main"`,
1920×1080, own `seg-NN.wav` + `window.segNN` captions). Rendered separately, ffmpeg-concat
into the final video. Every timed node: `data-start` + `data-duration` + `data-track-index`
+ `class="clip"`. Timeline `paused`, registered on `window.__timelines["main"]`.
Deterministic only — no `Date.now`/`Math.random`/`requestAnimationFrame` loop; three.js
renders inside the GSAP timeline `onUpdate`.
