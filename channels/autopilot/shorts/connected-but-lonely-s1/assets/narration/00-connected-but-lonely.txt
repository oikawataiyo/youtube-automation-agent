You open the app because you feel alone. And somehow, you close it lonelier than before. That is not a you problem. It is the design. Your brain evolved to read a small tribe. The feed hands it a thousand highlight reels. Everyone's best five seconds, edited past their own anxiety. So your mind does the math and decides everyone is happier, closer, more alive than you. But a view count is not a witness. A like is not a hand on your shoulder. A party you watched is not company. It is a window. And staring through enough windows makes your own room feel smaller. The fix is not more contact. It is realer contact. One message you actually send counts more than an hour of watching. So send this to the person you have been meaning to text.
